# GreenCo Sustainability Report 2023

## Climate Targets and Emissions

GreenCo commits to reducing absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions
by 42% by 2030, against a 2021 baseline year. Our long-term ambition is net zero
across Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions by 2050. In fiscal year 2023, total Scope 1
emissions were 1,250,000 tCO2e, down from 1,310,000 tCO2e in 2022.

Interim milestones include a 25% reduction by 2027. Progress is reported annually
and verified by an external auditor.

## Governance

The Sustainability Committee oversees climate strategy and reports to the Board of
Directors. The committee is chaired by the Chief Sustainability Officer. The Audit
Committee reviews the accuracy of disclosed emissions metrics.

## Risk and Compliance

GreenCo follows the TCFD framework for climate-related financial disclosures. All
sustainability claims must be supported by auditable evidence. The whistleblower
policy protects employees who report environmental or governance concerns through an
anonymous reporting channel, with explicit anti-retaliation provisions.

## Sustainable Finance

In 2023, GreenCo issued USD 500 million in green bonds to fund renewable energy
projects, including 300 MW of new solar capacity.
