# GreenCo Code of Conduct and Compliance Policy 2023

## Purpose

This policy sets out the standards of conduct required of all employees, contractors,
and suppliers. Compliance with this policy is mandatory.

## Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Employees shall not offer or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper payment. Gifts
exceeding USD 100 in value must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 30 days.

## Whistleblower Protection

Any person may report a suspected violation through the anonymous reporting channel.
The company prohibits retaliation against any whistleblower. Reports are reviewed by
the Audit Committee within 14 days.

## Data Protection

Personal data must be processed in accordance with applicable regulations. Access to
confidential records is restricted to authorized personnel only.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including
termination of employment or contract.
